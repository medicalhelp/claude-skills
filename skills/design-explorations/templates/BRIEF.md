## 4. Motion and craft bar (the senior designer's bar)
1. Motion explains where things came from and where they went.
2. One spring family by role: layout (no overshoot, e.g. stiffness 170 / damping 26), small confirmations (light bounce, 300 / 20), large surfaces (110 / 22), fades 150–250 ms ease-out. Entrances slower than exits.
3. Travel time scales with distance (about 280–520 ms across the screen), on a curve that slows down.
4. Stagger 60–90 ms in reading order.
5. Ambient elements never move faster than a slow breath unless reacting to input.
6. Everything is interruptible; every piece has a reduced-motion version (crossfades and highlights, no travel).
7. Every piece ends in a calm, correct resting state; anything that changes data has an undo; tap targets ≥ 44 pt; text ≥ 4.5:1.
8. A comment block with the sequence at 1× in every piece, so it can be rebuilt exactly.
